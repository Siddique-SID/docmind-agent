# Expense Policy
All business expenses must be submitted through the Expenses app within 30 days of purchase. Receipts are required for any single expense above 25 pounds. Expenses without receipts can only be claimed with written approval from a department head.
Travel is booked through the approved travel agency. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Hotels are capped at 150 pounds per night in the UK and 200 pounds per night abroad.
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 40 pounds per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Reimbursements are paid in the next monthly payroll cycle after approval.
