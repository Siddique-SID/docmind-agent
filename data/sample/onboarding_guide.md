# New Starter Onboarding Guide
On your first day, collect your laptop from the IT desk on the ground floor and set up your account with the IT team. Your buddy will schedule a 30-minute welcome call within the first two days.
During your first month you will complete mandatory security and data protection training in the learning portal. Your manager will set three objectives for your probation period, reviewed at the 90-day checkpoint.
The company runs an all-hands meeting on the first Thursday of each month. Office hours are 9:30am to 5:30pm, with hybrid working allowed up to three days per week in the office.
