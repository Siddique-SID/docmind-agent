# Leave Policy
Acme Labs employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, pro-rated for joiners after January. Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried over into the next year with manager approval. Public holidays are in addition to annual leave.
Sick leave is paid for up to 10 days per year. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note is required. Employees should notify their manager before 9am on the first day of absence.
Parental leave provides 16 weeks of fully paid leave for the primary carer and 4 weeks for the secondary carer. Requests must be submitted at least 8 weeks before the planned start date through the HR portal.
